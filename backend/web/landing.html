How it works

From “I need a saw”
to sawing, in an afternoon.

1

Find it next door

Search what neighbors within walking distance have listed. Photos, daily prices and owner ratings up front — exact addresses stay private until you're booked.

2

Book & pay in the app

Request the dates; the owner taps approve. Your card is charged for the rental and a refundable deposit is held, not taken — it backs the tool while it's in your hands.

3

Meet, make, return

Pick it up, build the thing, hand it back. Both sides confirm the handoff, the deposit hold is released, the owner gets paid, and you both leave a review.
